# Task 2: Complete Digital Marketing Plan for a Startup

## Project Overview
GlowNest is a fictional Pakistani skincare startup. This project presents a budget-friendly digital marketing plan to grow brand awareness and attract customers.

## Objectives
- Increase brand awareness.
- Improve social media engagement.
- Increase website traffic through SEO.
- Generate online sales.
- Build an email subscriber list.

## Target Audience
- Age: 18–30 years
- Location: Pakistan
- Interests: Skincare, beauty, and self-care

## Marketing Strategy

### 1. Social Media Marketing
- Platforms: Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.
- Publish skincare tips, reels, and product videos.
- Post consistently to increase engagement.

### 2. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- Research relevant keywords.
- Optimize website titles and product descriptions.
- Publish skincare blog posts.

### 3. Paid Advertising
- Use Facebook and Instagram advertisements.
- Test different creatives and audiences.
- Monitor advertising performance.

### 4. Email Marketing
- Send welcome emails.
- Share skincare tips and product updates.
- Announce special offers.

## Monthly Budget

| Activity | Budget |
|---|---:|
| Meta Ads | PKR 5,000 |
| Micro-influencer | PKR 2,000 |
| Contingency | PKR 1,000 |
| **Total** | **PKR 8,000** |

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Social media reach and engagement
- Follower growth
- Website traffic
- Email subscribers
- Sales and conversion rate

## Conclusion
GlowNest will use social media, SEO, paid advertising, and email marketing to build its online presence within a limited budget.

## Disclaimer
GlowNest is a fictional brand created for educational purposes.
